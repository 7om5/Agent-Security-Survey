B.2 Single-Agent Prevention Results

We further evaluate MASLEAK against three single-agent
prevention methods. As shown in Table 13, although these
defenses reduce extraction effectiveness to some extent (e.g.,

high extraction rates across all metrics. This indicates that
lightweight single-agent prevention methods are insufficient
to defend against our attack in MAS settings.
