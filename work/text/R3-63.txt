                      Introduction

models (MLLMs) (Bai et al. 2025b; Zhu et al. 2025; Xun

phone agents (Jiang et al. 2025; Ma, Zhang, and Zhao 2024;
Dai et al. 2025; Wang et al. 2025), users increasingly rely
on intelligent assistants to automate routine tasks such as
sending messages, ordering takeout, and online shopping.
