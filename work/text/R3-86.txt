                                                                                         A Open Science and Ethical Considerations

                                                                                         MetaMancer investigates a dual-use security risk in the tool selec-

                                                                                         tion of LLM agents. All experiments were conducted in controlled

                                                                                         benchmark environments without targeting deployed agent ser-

                                                                                         vices, third-party marketplaces, production APIs, or real users. No

                                                                                         harmful real-world tool actions were executed, and no private user

                                                                                         data, credentials, or personally identifiable information were ac-

                                                                                         cessed. By characterizing this attack surface and evaluating corre-

                                                                                         sponding defenses, including CVR, this study aims to improve the

                                                                                         security of tool-augmented LLM agents.
